NYC DCP Borough Boundaries

Knowledge resource

A governed public view of the NYC Department of City Planning (DCP) as a boundary-data publisher — its two Borough Boundaries catalog datasets and the ten governed measurements drawn from them. For how these measurements compare against the Census Bureau, OpenStreetMap, NYS Civil Boundaries, and other independently governed sources, see NYC Boundaries.

Validation and provenance

Borough area and perimeter figures are DCP's own published values, retrieved directly from the two Borough Boundaries catalog datasets; this page does not independently recompute them. Source-update timestamps are Socrata's own data-update values for each catalog dataset, not this page's own retrieval or review time. For independent cross-source verification of these figures, see NYC Boundaries.

Limitations

This page presents DCP's own published values as DCP asserts them. It does not independently verify DCP's geometry, and it is not the place to compare DCP against other sources — that comparison, including area agreement/difference against the Census Bureau, OpenStreetMap, and NYS Civil Boundaries, lives on NYC Boundaries.
