A public-source knowledge project

New York City, connected.

KnoEdg NYC is building a way to explore the city through connected, traceable public sources—from official data and archives to news, public websites, social media, and the people and organizations that shape New York.

The project is in early development. Public sources, methods, and releases will appear here as they are ready.
