NYC Knowledge Pack

The NYC Knowledge Pack is the structured foundation being developed for KnoEdg NYC.

It is designed to connect information while retaining where it came from, when it was observed, and the context and rights that accompany it.

Initial sources and releases will be published when they are ready for public use.
