About the project

A city is more than a dataset.

KnoEdg NYC is an independent, early-stage project for understanding New York City through its public information landscape.

That landscape spans official data, government publications, archives, research, journalism, public websites, social media, and the public activity of people and organizations.

The aim is to make connections easier to explore without flattening differences between sources. Publicly accessible information is not always openly licensed or freely redistributable; that distinction matters.
