About KnoEdg NYC

KnoEdg NYC brings together information about New York City from publicly available sources, making connections among the city’s places, people, organizations, events and systems easier to explore.

Sources may include official data, government publications, archives, research, journalism, public websites, social media, and public information shared by people and organizations.

Each source has its own purpose, perspective and limitations. KnoEdg NYC keeps those differences visible so visitors can see where information came from, when it was published and how it may have changed.

Public accessibility does not necessarily mean that information is openly licensed or freely redistributable. KnoEdg NYC respects and preserves those distinctions.

KnoEdg NYC is in early development. Its coverage and ways to explore the city will expand progressively.
