NYC Census Gazetteer Counties

Knowledge resource

A governed public view of the U.S. Census Bureau as a boundary-data publisher — its 2023 county Gazetteer file and the five governed land/water measurements drawn from it for the NYC-area counties. For how these measurements compare against DCP, OpenStreetMap, NYS Civil Boundaries, and other independently governed sources, see NYC Boundaries.

Dataset

The Census Bureau publishes an annual county Gazetteer file, a TIGER/Line-derived attribute table (not raw geometry) giving each county's land area, water area, and internal point. This page uses the 2023 vintage.

Limitations

This page presents the Census Bureau's own published values as published. It does not independently verify Census geometry, and it is not the place to compare Census against other sources — that comparison, including area agreement/difference against DCP, OpenStreetMap, and NYS Civil Boundaries, lives on NYC Boundaries. The Gazetteer file is an attribute table, not raw geometry — it carries no polygon boundary of its own.
