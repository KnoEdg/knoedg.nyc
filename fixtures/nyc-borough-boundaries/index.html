Published fixture set

NYC borough shoreline boundaries

This fixture set supplies the geometry used to render the five-borough map on the KnoEdg.NYC homepage. It represents the current New York City borough boundaries with water areas excluded, producing the recognizable land and shoreline shapes.

Included geometry

Manhattan, the Bronx, Brooklyn, Queens and Staten Island are included as five reviewed, preprojected SVG paths.

Publication path

The boundary assertions were governed in the private pack-nyc repository, cleared for publication through meta-knoedg-nyc, and published here as a public-safe fixture. The site generates the presentation SVG exclusively from that committed fixture, without network access or a private-repository dependency.

Fixture artifacts

  • Boundary fixture (JSON)
  • Offline SVG generator
  • Generated map (SVG)
  • SHA-256 checksum manifest

Upstream source

NYC Department of City Planning, Borough Boundaries, water areas excluded. Dataset identifier: gthc-hcne.

Reproducibility note

This first publication is reproducible from the reviewed public fixture forward: the fixture regenerates the SVG byte for byte. The original upstream API-response checksum and the initial geographic-to-SVG projection script were not retained. Future publication runs are required to preserve that complete upstream transformation chain.
