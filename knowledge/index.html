About the knowledge

Knowledge

KnoEdg NYC brings together knowledge about New York City from many different sources.

Rather than blending everything into a single account, it keeps each source visible—including its context, perspective, timing and limitations. This makes it possible to explore connections while also seeing where information agrees, differs or changes over time.

The project is beginning with selected information about New York City and will expand progressively as additional sources and subjects are reviewed.

Public accessibility does not necessarily permit information to be copied or redistributed. What KnoEdg NYC can display will depend on the rights and conditions associated with each source.
